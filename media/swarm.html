Start here

  1. Open and close the dashboard: click Swarm in the status bar (bottom left), the Rebel Bot Swarm icon in the activity bar (left edge), or press Ctrl+Alt+S. You are here now.
  2. Press OPEN (it shows your leader's name) to start work: a new Claude Code chat opens with the starter prompt. Press Enter, then say what you want to do.
  3. Close chat tabs when the job is handed over: the dashboard keeps watching your team.
  4. Save team to keep your pictures, names and settings safe (also before you uninstall).
